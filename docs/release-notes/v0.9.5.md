## 中文

### 修复

- 修复 macOS Quick Look 切换到桌面或其他应用后仍残留在屏幕上的问题。
- 权限引导现在会明确处理不支持的平台，避免点击后静默无响应。

### 改进

- macOS Quick Look 失活处理改用系统应用失活通知，减少后台轮询并提升窗口关闭可靠性。

## English

### Fixed

- Fixed Quick Look remaining on screen after switching away from ClipClop on macOS.
- Unsupported permission setup now reports a clear error instead of silently doing nothing.

### Improved

- macOS Quick Look deactivation now uses the system app deactivation notification instead of background polling.
